# Trim

The finish carpenter's stage, after Flooring. Data in `src/trim.js`, items in `src/sim/content/trim.js`, order and gestures in `src/sim/content/construction.js`.

## Order (support rules)

1. **Window stool**: the sill board, ears past the casing.
2. **Casing**: legs and head on every door and window (needs the stools). Craftsman and Farmhouse butt a head over the legs, Craftsman adds a cap; Colonial and Modern mitre at 45°; Modern runs a picture frame under the window instead of a stool.
3. **Apron** under each stool (needs the casing).
4. **Baseboard**: runs along every wall, stopping at the door casing; long walls take more than one length (needs the casing).
5. **Shoe** along hard floors (Colonial), then **crown** at the ceiling (Colonial).
6. **Paint**: caulk the seams, fill the nail holes, paint. Painted styles change colour when this is done.

## Styles

| Style | Casing | Base | Extras | Finish |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Craftsman | 1x4 flat, butt head + cap | 1x6 | stool | stained fir |
| Colonial | moulded, mitred | 3 1/4" | stool, shoe, crown | primed, painted white |
| Modern | thin MDF, mitred | square MDF | picture-frame windows | painted |
| Farmhouse | wide rough-sawn 1x6, butt | 1x8 | stool | stained |

Every piece is cut on the mitre saw and brad-nailed (about one brad every 40 cm). Trim at a door head is reached from the floor; crown takes the ladder.
