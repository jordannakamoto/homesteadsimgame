Controls

Mouse
Turn the camera. Attacks go where it faces
Q and E
Twist your body left or right. Twisting with a cut as it swings drives it faster and harder. Alternate driven cuts, left then right then left, to build flow
W A S D
Move. Stepping into a blow adds weight
Tap left
Quick cuts and a thrust. A jab from behind the guard
Hold left
Aim a heavy blow like the bow: a mark shows where it will land. Turn to aim it, push or pull the mouse to set the reach, hold for power, release to strike. Close is a chop, middle a slash, far a thrust
Wheel, or R
Stance. In for melee, out for the bow
Hold right
Melee stance: guard, and parry if raised just before a yellow attack lands. Bow stance: raise the bow and aim. Hold Q to pull the string, let go to loose. A fuller pull flies faster, flatter and further. Stand still to loose a steady shot; one loosed on the move wanders
F
Off hand: shove, or throw anyone off balance. Twist as you grab for a hip throw
Shift
Hold to sprint
Shift + Space
Roll
Hold C
Crouch. Enemies notice you from much closer, and a hit on one that hasn't seen you is a sneak attack
Space
Jump, and once more in the air
Left, in the air
Down attack. Landing on an enemy bounces you back up
Alt + tap, Alt + Q
Dash strike, or triple shot while aiming
1 to 4, Esc, M
Weapons or a terrain tool. Free the mouse. Sound on or off
